Block Max – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Max ("the App"), an Android puzzle game, handles information when you use it. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about information processed through the App and its advertising services.

2. Information That May Be Collected

The App uses advertising and related services, including Google Mobile Ads, to display advertisements and support the game. Depending on your device, settings, and use of the App, the App or its service providers may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Approximate location: This may be inferred from information such as your device's IP address.
  • App interactions: Information about interactions with the App and advertisements, such as ad views and engagement.
  • Diagnostics: Technical information used to identify errors, crashes, and performance problems.
  • Device or other IDs: Device identifiers and advertising identifiers used for advertising, measurement, and security.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Analytics and understanding how the App is used.
  • Monitoring and improving App performance.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity.
  • Maintaining security and complying with applicable requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

Some information may be collected by or shared with third-party service providers, including Google and advertising partners, to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, perform analytics, and help prevent fraud. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell personal information that you directly provide to us. However, third-party advertising services may collect and process information as described in their own policies.

5. Data Security

We use services and reasonable practices intended to protect information. Information transmitted by the App to service providers is protected in transit where supported by those services. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Block Max does not require users to create an account. Information collected by third-party advertising and service providers is retained according to their own retention policies.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information under our control, contact us using the email address below. You may also manage advertising privacy settings or reset your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Requests concerning information held by a third-party provider may need to be made directly to that provider.

7. Children's Privacy

Block Max is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you have a concern about information relating to a child, please contact us.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own privacy policies. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. Any update will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
